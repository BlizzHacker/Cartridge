3-Minute Setup Guide (Zero Tech Skills Needed)

Everything happens over Wi-Fi — you don't even need a USB cable between your PC and the TV box.

1

Turn On Developer Mode on Your TV

  1. On your Onn 4K / Google TV, open Settings → System → About.
  2. Scroll to Android TV OS build and click OK 7 times until it says "You are now a developer!".
  3. Go back one screen → open Developer options → turn USB debugging ON.
2

Run MoveWeight-TV-Setup.exe

  1. Double-click MoveWeight-TV-Setup.exe on your Windows PC.
  2. Pick your TV box from the auto-scanned Wi-Fi dropdown.
  3. Check which Top 10 streaming apps to keep (Plex, Jellyfin, Netflix, Prime, Paramount+, etc.).
  4. Enter your RomM URL + Username/Password (optional) and click SET UP MY TV BOX.
  5. When your TV asks "Allow USB debugging?", click Always allow → Allow.
3

Pair Xbox Controllers & Play!

  1. On your TV, open Settings → Remotes & Accessories → Pair remote or accessory.
  2. Turn on your Xbox Wireless Controller and hold the small Pair button on top for 3 seconds until it flashes fast.
  3. Select Xbox Wireless Controller → Pair. Repeat for up to 4 controllers!
  4. Open Cartridge, Argosy, Lemuroid, or Yarr.It and enjoy!
